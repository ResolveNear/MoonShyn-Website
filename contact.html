Frequently Asked Questions

To initiate a return, process it through our returns portal using your order number and email address. We accept returns within 30 days of the delivery date. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have original tags attached.

Currently, we ship within the US and to Canada. We are working hard to expand our international shipping options in the near future! Join our newsletter to be notified when we launch worldwide shipping.

Our matte satin pieces are delicate. We recommend hand washing in cold water with mild detergent or machine washing on a delicate cycle in a mesh laundry bag. Lay flat to dry out of direct sunlight. Never tumble dry. Use a cool iron on the reverse side if necessary.
